What to expect

RootCause is provided “as is” during early access, without a service-level guarantee. Automated log analysis may be incomplete or incorrect. Alerts can be delayed or may not arrive, and RootCause may miss errors. Verify important findings against your systems.

Monitoring and alerts

24/7 monitoring and alert delivery are planned Prime features and are not live during early access. When launched, RootCause will attempt to process incoming logs and send alerts, but the service, hosting, and email or chat providers may delay or miss them. Never use RootCause as your only monitor for critical systems.

Availability and recovery

The application currently runs on Render’s Free hosting plan, which can go idle after inactivity and may take time to wake. The database’s instant-restore history is limited to about six hours. If an error is missed, a problem could go unnoticed for six hours or more, and data older than the available restore window may not be recoverable.

Your account and data

You are responsible for protecting your password and app keys, choosing what logs to submit, and maintaining any additional monitoring or backups you need. Do not send passwords, tokens, or unnecessary personal information in logs. You can delete your account from the workspace.

Third-party services

RootCause depends on Render, Neon, and configured email or notification providers. If AI explanations are enabled, unmatched redacted log excerpts may be processed by the configured AI provider, Groq or OpenAI. If paid billing is enabled, Razorpay processes subscription checkout and payments. Their availability, delivery times, terms, and data practices are outside RootCause’s control.

Paid plans

Paid checkout is disabled during early access. If Plus billing is enabled, Plus is a recurring monthly subscription at the price and tax amount shown in Razorpay Checkout. It renews monthly until cancelled. You can request cancellation from Billing and payments; access continues through the already-paid period.

Acceptable use

Do not use RootCause to violate applicable law, interfere with other services, or submit malicious content. You remain responsible for your applications and for actions taken based on RootCause suggestions.
