# Brag Plan: RootCause

## What is this app?
RootCause turns one confusing error or stack trace into a plain-English explanation, likely cause, and practical next steps — for small teams who would rather ship than babysit dashboards.

## The angle
"Your app is talking. We're listening." The video is the satisfying flip: raw panic (`connect ECONNREFUSED 127.0.0.1:5432`) becomes calm guidance ("Checkout cannot connect to Postgres"). Live now: explain one error. Coming soon: security review and 24/7 monitoring — labeled honestly.

## Hook (first 2.5 seconds)
A terminal error stamps onto the deep forest green canvas:
`ERROR checkout-api · connect ECONNREFUSED 127.0.0.1:5432 · HTTP 500`
Huge type lands: **Know what broke.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Explain (Live now):** The error morphs into the product's sample explainer — "Checkout cannot connect to Postgres." + three check-next steps. Caption: "Paste one error. Get the why and the fix."
- **Monitoring tease (Coming soon):** Hero dashboard waveform + incident card "Retries are climbing on checkout." Caption: "When it breaks, know before your users do." Soft "Coming soon" pill.

## Outro / punchline
**r.** rootcause mark stamps in.
"Know what broke. Know what to do next."
"Try free · 5 explanations a day."

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: raw error / stack paste (`ECONNREFUSED 127.0.0.1:5432`)
2. Key action: RootCause explains in plain English
3. Result: clear next steps (and a glimpse of planned monitoring)

## Audio direction
- Role: Warm chillhop bed; tactile UI accents on reveals
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (~110 BPM)
- Music treatment: Start near 0s, bed ~0.22–0.28 volume, soft fade-in 0.4s, fade-out last 1.2s
- Music cue guidance: Preset cues read (~109.96 BPM). Strong cues in window: 15.82, 18.01, 18.55, 20.19. Scene cuts prefer beat grid: 0.27 / 2.46 / 8.22 / 14.73 / 18.55. Sequential text holds every other beat — readability over grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; sage glow / panel presence breathes with RMS/bass — no waveform bars
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched, polished restraint
